# Elara Access Packet

- Route status: `alternate_aligned`
- Completion target: `60%` before any new expansion beyond Elara's first decision point is allowed
- Parent canonical beat: `hushbriar_elara_resolution`
- Allowed entry beat: the canonical post-Moonwell aftermath clue path remains the default; earlier hideout contact may exist as low-context ambient tension, but Elara herself must still be earned through trust or breach
- Allowed cities: `Hushbriar`
- Named NPC rules: `Elara`, `Neala`, and `Liobhan` must keep the identities, goals, and emotional roles assigned by the campaign draft and canon notes; no helperfication, no softened motives, no route-role swapping

## Purpose

Use this packet to constrain alternate hideout access and later Elara expansion without reintroducing the deleted holdfast-management branch.

## Guardrails

- Must not replace or weaken the canonical town, inn, Fionnlagh, screams, Moonwell, and aftermath sequence
- Must not turn the hideout into a map hub, rest hub, or management loop
- Must not rewrite Neala or Liobhan into exposition guides or party managers
- Must not change Elara's dilemma, goals, or the moral pressure around her blood
- Must not reopen `Solasmor` or `Soul Mill` travel from Elara-facing scenes before their own canon-safe entry beats exist
- Hostile access must come through breach or coercive entry, not through the guild suddenly becoming cooperative for convenience

## Required Before Promotion

- Preserve one canonical route from aftermath to Elara and the Stone decision
- Keep hostile/coercive hideout access convergent with the canonical Elara encounter rather than splitting into a separate campaign path
- Add tests proving early hideout contact stays low-yield, cooperative access still requires earned trust, and hostile access does not collapse into escort behavior
