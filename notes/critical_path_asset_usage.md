# Critical Path Asset Usage

This note anchors the intended image progression for the early critical path so future scene rewrites do not drift away from the current tone pass.

- `landscapes/silverthorn_market_avenue.png`
  Use for Silverthorn city-center, market, temple, inn, notice-board, and gate-adjacent city scenes.

- `landscapes/forest_walk.png`
  Use for the first healthy Shadowmire road beat and other still-living forest travel before corruption becomes visible.

- `landscapes/foggy_forest.png`
  Use for the first omen beat in Shadowmire, where the air turns strange and the purple haze begins to gather.

- `landscapes/dying_bird_scene.png`
  Use for the bird-fall omen scene only, or for similarly specific plague-warning beats.

- `landscapes/sporefall_crimson_frontier.png`
  Use immediately after the blackout and for first-view Sporefall frontier scenes under the crimson moon.

- `landscapes/forest_walk_alt.png`
  Keep as a generic forest/glade fallback. Do not use it for the first Shadowmire omen sequence unless a scene explicitly returns to a still-living forest mood.

- `landscapes/silverthorn_market_avenue.png` is still serving as a temporary fallback for some Hushbriar scenes.
  Those scenes need dedicated Hushbriar art later, but the writing should continue to carry the emotional weight in the meantime.
