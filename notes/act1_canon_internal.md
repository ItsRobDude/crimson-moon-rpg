# Act I Canon Note (Internal)

This note is for contributors only.

Do not surface these truths in player-facing codex text, menu copy, or early-scene exposition unless the story has already earned the reveal.

## Place Truth

- `Whisperwood Borough` and `Sporefall` are the same place.
- `Whisperwood` is the pre-ritual name.
- `Sporefall` is the post-ritual name used after the catastrophe.
- In present-tense horror scenes, `Sporefall` is the correct register.
- In memory fragments, older records, and pre-ritual references, `Whisperwood` is the correct register.

## Ritual Truth

- The cathedral is ground zero for the failed ritual and plague breakout.
- Liam did not knowingly volunteer to become a plague host.
- The ritual was tainted, and Liam's body became the host for a forming portal into the Underdark.
- The plague burst from Liam's body during the ritual collapse.
- Aodhan's last-ditch spell slowed the spread, sealed the borough behind a magical containment field, and held Liam in stasis while Aodhan went searching for the relic/stone.
- The `Crimson Moon` is not a passive omen. It is a powerful spell of Ciara's, a watchful sacrificial force that strengthens offerings beneath its gaze.

## Silverthorn Opening Knowledge

At the start of the game, Silverthorn does not know the hidden ritual truth above.

What the city does know or suspect:

- A powerful relic has been found in `Durnhelm`.
- The relic is politically explosive, and every city is anxious about who should hold it.
- The discovery has deepened mistrust between realms and races.
- Whisperwood has seemingly vanished after the ritual.
- Rumors of war, sabotage, plague, and relic theft are spreading.
- Alderic knows more than he admits and is already moving in concert with Ciara, but this must remain subtext early on.

Player-facing early knowledge should stay narrow:

- dwarves found a powerful relic
- the northern road is wrong
- Whisperwood has "disappeared"
- people fear looming war and political fracture

## Character Guardrails

- `Aodhan` must remain morally unresolved: grief-stricken, reactive, dangerous, and impossible to sort neatly into hero or villain.
- `Neala` and `Liobhan` are not early-route content. They belong to the later bypass route that reaches the Viridian Forest / Hushbriar thread.
- `Eoin` is a ghostlike, displaced survivor who does not understand the full truth of his own state.
- Eoin's mother is dead beneath the north bridge, mummified and overtaken by the spore.

## Protected Emotional Set Pieces

These scenes should be adapted carefully and not flattened into quest utility:

- Eoin and the north-bridge thread
- Moonwell / Aodhan by the bodies
- Lament Hill / the witch

## Tone Guardrail

Post-blackout Sporefall is grim, foreboding, morbid, and explicit.

The body horror can be direct and M-rated.
The writing should still preserve restraint in revelation pacing: the player should feel the truth pressing in before the full explanation is spoken aloud.
