# Narrative Tone Audit

This is a narrow audit of the highest-risk story clusters after the recent authored-scene expansion.

| Cluster | Status | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Silverthorn briefing / prep / rumor surfaces | `minor drift` | The opening pressure is strong, but future additions here are at the highest risk of sounding merely orderly or politically tense instead of death-haunted and physically uneasy. |
| Shadowmire collapse and Sporefall arrival | `aligned` | The road-to-blackout sequence stays sensory, bodily, and concrete, with omen and corruption arriving through dead wildlife, wrong air, and physical distress. |
| Eoin and north-bridge material | `aligned` | Eoin's voice stays frightened and young, while the bridge thread preserves the personal, bodily ugliness that makes the borough feel inhabited by loss rather than generic ruin. |
| Moonwell and Aodhan | `aligned` | The scene keeps grief, child death, and catastrophic consequence foregrounded without flattening Aodhan into a speechy villain. |
| Durnhelm and Cathal | `minor drift` | The current material lands well, but this route will drift fast if later city beats become neutral shop or hub content instead of aftermath, burial, and civic damage. |
| Lament Hill and Aine | `aligned` | The hill, the accusation, and Aine's reveal all retain grief-haunting, accusation, and brutality close to the campaign draft's emotional weight. |
| Hushbriar, Fionnlagh, guild, and Elara | `aligned` | Occupied dread, refugee pressure, ugly compromise, and hunted vulnerability stay visible throughout the route. |
| Archives and Thalion | `minor drift` | The penitential severity is present, but future additions here could become too grand or polished if spectacle starts outrunning guilt, warning, and ritual cost. |
