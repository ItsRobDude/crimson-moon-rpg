Scene Choices

Checks And Consequences

Most checks are `d20 + ability mod + proficiency`. Failure usually redirects or costs time instead of ending the route outright.

Silverthorn Time

City actions can consume time. One or two strong preparations are often better than trying to see every street before the eastern road.

Combat Rhythm

Your turn usually means one action, often a bonus action, and movement. Read the turn summary first, then decide whether this round wants pressure, repositioning, or utility.

Saving And Partial Systems

The game auto-saves on scene change. Manual save is in the menu. Ability score improvements work at level-up; feat choice is not fully surfaced in this build yet.

Take a Rest

Rest trades time for recovery. The safest choice is not always the best one for the road ahead.

Short Rest (1 Hour)

Recover a small amount of health. Some abilities may be restored.

Long Rest (8 Hours)

Fully recover health, spell slots, and all abilities. Advances time significantly.
